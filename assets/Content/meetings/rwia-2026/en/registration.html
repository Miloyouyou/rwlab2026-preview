Registration

Online registration is open. Conference check-in is on December 11, 08:00–20:00. Delegates may submit their details through the conference registration system.

Applications for contributed presentations are open

Researchers, engineers, early-career academics and students are welcome to present recent research, engineering practice and application case studies in robotic welding, intelligent manufacturing and related fields.

Prospective presenters should email the presentation title, an abstract in Chinese or English, name, affiliation and contact details to the secretariat by November 30, 2026, and complete conference registration. Research, engineering and application presentations are welcome. Inclusion, format and time slot are subject to committee review and the final program.

Presentation applications: rwlab21@sjtu.edu.cn

Accommodation

Conference rate: CNY 400 per room per night. Hotel reservations close on November 30, 2026; rates and availability may change thereafter.

If you need the conference team to assist with a room booking, select that option in the registration form and confirm with the secretariat.

Arranging your participation

  1. Complete the online registration form and confirm your delegate category and fee.
  2. Indicate any accommodation needs and confirm invoicing and lodging with the secretariat.
  3. Payment instructions are in the outline program and registration document. Verify the details with the secretariat before paying.
