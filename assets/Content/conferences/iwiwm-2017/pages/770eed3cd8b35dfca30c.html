Route from the Airport/Railway to the Grand Mercure Shanghai Century Park

Grand Mercure Shanghai Century Park, formerly known as Shanghai Lisheng Hotel, is located in the Shanghai Pudong New District. The traffic is convenient due to its superior geographical position. The hotel gives a new definition of fashion and elegance with a refined and minimalist modernist style. Strong color art everywhere in hotel accompanies you to the hotel from the lobby, and various modern art works on display in the hotel play an important role. There are spacious and comfortable rooms and suites including spacious french windows, plasma flat TV, et al. Each room is equipped with a desk, guests can also enjoy free high-speed Internet access and the hotel’s ad hoc private computer interface.
