Keynote/Invited Speaker


Dr. Baicun WANG

University of Michigan & Chinese Academy of Engineering


Biography

    Dr. Wang now is a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Michigan supported by “The International Postdoctoral Exchange Fellowship Program”. His main research interests focus on intelligent manufacturing and development strategy.
    Dr. Baicun Wang received Ph.D. degree in Chemical Process Equipment from Zhejiang University (ZJU) in 2016. During 2014-2015, he was supported by the program of China Scholarship Council (CSC) as a joint Ph.D. student in Georgia Institute of Technology (GT).
