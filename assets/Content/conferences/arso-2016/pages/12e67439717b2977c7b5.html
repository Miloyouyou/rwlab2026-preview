The registration fee covers the following:

  • For Full Registration:
  • 2 Paper presentations, 1 Conference bag, 1 CD/USB Proceedings, 1 Welcome Reception ticket, 3 Lunch tickets, 1 Banquet tickets, 1 Farewell Reception ticket,Free visit of CIROS 2016 and SJTU Lab. Drinks and food served at the tea-breaks are also free to all registered attendees.
  • For Student Registration:
  • 1 Paper presentation, 1 Conference bag, 1 CD/USB Proceedings, 1 Welcome Reception ticket, 3 Lunch tickets, 1 Banquet tickets, 1 Farewell Reception ticket,Free visit of CIROS 2016 and SJTU Lab. Drinks and food served at the tea-breaks are also free to all registered attendees.
  • For IEEE Life Member Registration:
  • Free of charge
  1. One full registration covers a maximum of two accepted papers by the same registered author.
  2. One student registration covers a maximum of one accepted papers by the same registered author.
  3. An additional RMB1500 must be paid by the same author for each additional paper to be presented at the conference and included in the proceedings if the additional paper(s) have no other registered authors.
  4. For papers with more than 6 pages, the author needs to pay RMB650/page for the extra pages to be in the conference proceedings.
