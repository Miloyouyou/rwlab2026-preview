Invited guests



Prof. Jinping QU

Guangdong Mechanical Engineering Society, China


Biography

    Jin Jinping, male, from Huangmei County, was born in June 1957. Member of the Communist Party of China, Doctor of Engineering, Professor, Doctoral Supervisor, National Science Fund for Outstanding Youth, First Distinguished Professor of the Yangtze River Scholars Award Program of the Ministry of Education, National Key Discipline Leader of Materials Processing Engineering of South China University of Technology, Guangdong Mechanical Engineering Society Chairman of the Board of Directors and Academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering.     Mainly engaged in the research and teaching of polymer material molding processing technology and equipment and its theory, the first to propose plastic vibration shear deformation and volume tensile deformation dynamic molding method and principle, the system developed the polymer material processing theory, invention and successful development A series of new technologies and equipment for plastic molding. He has obtained a series of patents for technical inventions: more than 20 invention patents in China, and 3 invention patents in more than 10 countries and regions such as the United States and Europe; he has presided over more than 30 national key science and technology projects and related engineering supporting projects. More than 20 results have been identified, among which a number of achievements have been identified as: technology is at the international leading level; more than 100 papers have been published in three major indexes, and five books have been published; the results of industrialization have achieved remarkable results, and the application of new technologies has produced a huge economy. Benefits and social benefits.
