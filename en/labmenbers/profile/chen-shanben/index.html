Shanben Chen

Founder and Director | Professor, Doctoral Supervisor

sbchen@sjtu.edu.cn

Biography

Shanben Chen is a Professor and doctoral supervisor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and the founder and director of the Intelligentized Robotic Welding Technology Laboratory. He established the laboratory in March 2000. His research integrates robotic welding perception, decision-making and control, with a focus on welding dynamics and complex-system modelling.

Research & achievements

  • Intelligent robotic welding：Visual, acoustic and multisensor perception for welding-environment recognition, weld-pool feature extraction and process monitoring.
  • Welding dynamics and quality control：Fuzzy-neural and self-learning control, and multimodal modelling for penetration, bead formation and defect prediction.
  • Autonomous robots and complex systems：Locally autonomous welding robots, flexible manufacturing systems and robust control under uncertainty.
