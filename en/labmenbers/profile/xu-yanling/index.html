Yanling Xu

Associate Professor, Doctoral Supervisor

ylxu@sjtu.edu.cn

Biography

Yanling Xu is an Associate Professor and doctoral supervisor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Her work covers intelligent robotic welding, machine vision and multisensor quality monitoring, together with digital-twin-assisted programming, wire-arc additive manufacturing and remanufacturing.

Research & achievements

  • Visual perception and autonomous welding：3D point-cloud localization, weld-profile extraction and autonomous seam tracking, including teaching-free positioning and visual guidance.
  • Multisensor fusion and quality inspection：Welding-defect detection using visual and process signals, with cross-condition transfer and small-sample learning to improve adaptability.
  • Digital twins and additive–subtractive manufacturing：Automatic robot programming, wire-arc additive manufacturing and remanufacturing, and grinding-process modelling and optimization.
