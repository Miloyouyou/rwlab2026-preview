Biography

Zidong Yang is a master’s student at the Institute of Intelligent Welding and Precision Materials Manufacturing, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Zidong Yang studies intelligent prediction of robotic welding process parameters and data-driven modelling. The work focuses on process-data augmentation, ensemble prediction and prediction software development under limited-data conditions, alongside participation in the group’s research and industry collaboration projects.

Research interests

  • Intelligent welding processes
