Huabin Chen

Professor, Doctoral Supervisor

hbchen@sjtu.edu.cn

Biography

Huabin Chen is a Professor and doctoral supervisor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research combines process physics, data modelling and robotic system integration for intelligent welding and thermal manufacturing in complex environments.

Research & achievements

  • 3D perception and welding-path planning：Multi-view point-cloud reconstruction, path generation for complex intersecting joints and multi-pass welding, including LLM-assisted pre-weld perception and TCP pose generation.
  • Multimodal quality monitoring and process modelling：Visual, acoustic and process signals for defect recognition, penetration prediction and parameter modelling, combining physics-based constraints with data-driven methods.
  • Intelligent thermal and additive manufacturing：Laser directed energy deposition, multisensor monitoring and ultrasound-assisted manufacturing, with emphasis on forming stability, microstructure and mechanical properties.
